// About Us

Who's behind LevelX

A small crew, a shared channel, and a simple rule: every upload has to earn its level.

LevelX is a channel made by Record Team (R.T) — a small crew focused on recording, editing, and putting out content people actually want to watch.

The channel is owned by Karim Mohamed, who leads the team and steers what LevelX puts out.

Everything you see on LevelX is built on desktop under the project name levelxV@ — from raw capture to final cut.
